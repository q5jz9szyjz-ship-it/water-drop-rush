Clean Water Game

Water Drop Rush 💧

Collect clean water and help make a difference!

How To Play

Click the blue water drop to get 10 points. Avoid the dirty water because you lose 10 points. Get 100 points before the timer runs out!

Score: 0

Time: 30s

Press Start to play!

Why Clean Water Matters

Millions of people around the world need access to clean water. This game was inspired by charity: water and their mission to help people get clean and safe drinking water.
